Independent T-Test
Groups: Exactly 2
Question: Is μ₁ ≠ μ₂?
Stat: t = (x̄₁ − x̄₂) / SE
df: N − 2
Note: F = t² when there are 2 groups
Post-hoc: Not needed
One-Way ANOVA
Groups: 3 or more
Question: Is at least one μ different?
Stat: F = MSbetween / MSwithin
df: (k − 1, N − k)
Note: One test keeps α at .05
Post-hoc: Tukey (equal var.), Games-Howell

Which antibiotic clears the infection fastest?

Patients with a bacterial skin infection were randomly assigned to one of three antibiotics. Researchers recorded the number of days until each patient's infection cleared (fewer days = better). A few patients dropped out, so the groups are different sizes.

Is the mean number of days to recovery the same for all three antibiotics?

Factor: antibiotic (3 groups) · Response: days until infection cleared · H₀: μA = μB = μC · Hₐ: at least one mean is different
