Independent T-Test
Groups: Exactly 2
Question: Is M₁ ≠ M₂?
Stat: t = (M₁−M₂) / SE
df: N − 2
Note: F = t² when df₁ = 1
Post-hoc: Not needed
One-Way ANOVA
Groups: 3 or more
Question: Are ANY means ≠?
Stat: F = MS_between / MS_within
df: (k−1, N−k)
Note: One test protects α
Post-hoc: Tukey, Bonferroni…
